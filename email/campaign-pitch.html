Why I'm Running

My name's Aidan, and I'm running for IOP Comms Director.

I've always believed that the real value of the IOP comes from the excellent work our programs and members do every day, not from SAC or Exec. That's why IOP Comms should be meeting our members where the real work is happening: within our programs.

As Marketing Director at the HPR, I've experienced firsthand how hard it is to work within the current system. I know this is a common experience within the IOP, especially among smaller programs, but it shouldn't be.

I'm proposing four common-sense changes to make your lives easier, to improve communication within and between programs, and to create a better IOP community.

My Platform
#1
Programs First
Equal comms support for all 17 programs and 4 coalitions. Spotlighting smaller programs. A program spotlight series, member highlights, and an all-program presentation night with food and merch.
#2
OneIOP
One internal site for every announcement, event, and contact. Find anything in one click and get fewer emails while you're at it. Try the demo →
#3
Comms Playbook
A shared brand template, custom templates for every program, and comms training with office hours, so no program starts from zero.
#4
Member Perks
Program-specific merch, new member welcome kits, and photos and video back within 24 hours.
